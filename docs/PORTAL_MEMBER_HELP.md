# FaithFlow AI — Member Help Guide

> **For church members using the portal.**
> Plain-language answers to everyday questions. No technical knowledge required.

---

## Getting Started

### How do I get into the portal?

1. Go to your church's portal link (your admin will share this — it usually looks like `yourchurch.faithflow.ai/portal`).
2. You'll see a sign-in screen. Enter your email and password, or sign in with Google if your church has that set up.
3. If your email is already in your church's records, you'll go straight in.
4. If not, you'll see a short form asking for your name, email, and church. Fill it in and submit — a staff member will approve your request, usually within a day or two.

---

### I submitted an access request. What happens next?

Your church staff will see your request and approve it. Once approved, come back to the portal, sign in, and you'll have full access. You don't need to do anything else — just wait for the green light.

---

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the sign-in screen, click **"Forgot password?"** and follow the steps. A reset link will be sent to your email. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.

---

### I signed in but the portal won't open — it's asking me to "select a church."

This usually means your account isn't connected to a church organisation yet. Use the selector shown on screen to choose your church. If your church isn't listed, contact your church admin — they may need to add you to the right organisation.

## Your Profile

### What is my profile for?

Your profile is your record in the church system. It shows your contact details and controls what other members can see about you in the directory.

---

### How do I update my contact details?

1. Go to **Profile** in the top menu (or the person icon on mobile).
2. Edit your preferred name, phone number, or address.
3. Click **Save**.

> Your first name, last name, and email are managed by your church admin. If those need changing, contact your admin directly.

---

### What is a "preferred name"?

It's the name you actually go by — a nickname, a shortened name, or anything you prefer to be called. Once you set it, it appears everywhere in the portal instead of your first name (e.g. in the directory, in messages, and in the welcome header).

---

### What does my engagement score mean?

It's a read-only score your church uses internally to track involvement — based on things like attendance and activity. You can see it on your profile, but you can't change it. It's just for your own reference.

---

### Who can see my details?

You control this completely. On your Profile page, you'll find a **Privacy** section where you can:

- Set yourself as **Public** (visible in the member directory) or **Private** (hidden from the directory entirely)
- Individually hide your email, phone number, address, or photo — even if your listing is Public

Church staff and admins can always see your full details, regardless of these settings. Privacy controls only apply to what **other members** see.

---

## Directory

### What is the Directory?

The Directory is a list of your fellow church members. Think of it as a congregation contact book — useful for connecting with people in your church community.

---

### Why can't I see some members in the directory?

Members who have set their listing to **Private** won't appear. Members who have hidden specific fields (like email or phone) will show with those fields blank. Everyone's privacy choices are respected.

---

### Why am I not showing up in the directory?

Check your Profile → Privacy settings. If your **Directory visibility** is set to **Private**, you won't appear. Switch it to **Public** and save to become visible.

---

## Events

### How do I see what's coming up?

Go to **Events** in the navigation. You'll see a list of upcoming events with dates, times, and locations.

---

### How do I RSVP for an event?

Find the event and click **RSVP**. That's it — you're registered. Your RSVP is confirmed immediately.

---

### Some events have a form when I register. Do I have to fill it all in?

Questions marked with a **\*** are required — you won't be able to submit without them. The rest are optional. Just fill in what applies to you.

---

### An event costs money. How do I pay?

When you click **Register** on a paid event, you'll be taken to a secure payment page (Stripe or your church's local payment provider). Enter your card details, complete the payment, and you'll be brought back to the portal with your registration confirmed.

---

### How do I cancel my registration?

Find your registration in the events list and click **Cancel registration**. This removes you from the event immediately.

> If you paid for a ticket, speak to your church admin about a refund. Cancelling on the portal removes your spot but doesn't automatically process a refund.

---

### What do the registration status labels mean?

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Confirmed** | You're registered and all set |
| **Pending** | Waiting for payment or admin confirmation |
| **Cancelled** | Registration was cancelled |
| **Waitlisted** | Event is full — you're on the waiting list |

## Messages

### How do I send a message?

1. Go to **Messages** in the navigation.
2. To message someone for the first time, use the **"Start new conversation"** dropdown, pick the person, and click **Start**.
3. Type your message and click **Send**.

---

### Can I message church staff?

Yes. Staff members appear in the conversation list if they've been set up in the system. You can also start a conversation with any staff member from the new conversation dropdown.

---

### How do I attach a file to a message?

Below the message box, you'll see a file picker. Click it, choose your file, and wait for it to upload. Once it shows in the attachment list, send your message as normal.

**What you can attach:** Images (JPG, PNG, GIF), PDFs, Word documents, and plain text files.
**Size limit:** 10 MB per file.

---

### I can see "X is typing…" — what does that mean?

It means the other person in the conversation is currently typing a reply. It disappears when they stop or send their message.

---

### How do I know if someone read my message?

Below the message thread you'll see a "Last read" note showing when the other person last opened the conversation.

---

## Volunteer

### How do I sign up to serve?

1. Go to **Volunteer** in the navigation.
2. Browse the list of open shifts.
3. Find one that works for you and click **Sign up**.

You'll be added to the roster instantly.

---

### How do I cancel a shift I signed up for?

Find the shift in your "My shifts" list and click **Cancel**. You'll be removed from the roster right away. If it's close to the date, consider letting your ministry leader know directly.

---

### What's the difference between Roles and Shifts?

- **Roles** are the types of service positions your church has (e.g. "Greeter", "Sound team", "Children's ministry helper").
- **Shifts** are the specific scheduled times for each role. You sign up for a shift, not a role.

---

### How do I tell the church when I'm generally available?

Go to the **Availability** section under Volunteer:

1. Choose a day of the week.
2. Set the times you're free.
3. Add a note if needed (e.g. "Not during school term").
4. Click **Save**.

You can add as many availability windows as you like, and delete any you no longer need. This helps your church plan rosters around when people are actually free.

---

## Notifications

### Where do my notifications go?

Go to **Notifications** (or the bell icon on mobile). Your inbox shows all messages sent to you by your church — announcements, reminders, and other updates.

---

### How do I mark a notification as read?

Click **Mark read** next to the notification. Read notifications stay in your list but appear faded so you can tell them apart from new ones.

---

### How do I stop getting emails / texts / WhatsApp messages?

1. Go to **Notifications**.
2. Scroll to the channel preferences section.
3. Toggle off whichever channel you want to stop.

You can turn off any combination of **Email, SMS, WhatsApp, In-App,** and **Push** notifications — your choice.

---

### Can I set quiet hours so I don't get messages at night?

Yes. In the notification preferences, turn on **Quiet hours** for any channel and set your start and end time (e.g. 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM). Messages sent during that window will be held and delivered when your quiet period ends.

---

## Surveys

### I see a survey — do I have to fill it in?

That's up to you and your church, but surveys are the easiest way to share your thoughts with leadership. They're usually short and your responses help your church plan better.

---

### How do I submit a survey?

1. Answer each question. Required questions are marked with **\***.
2. Once all required questions are answered, the **Submit** button will turn active.
3. Click **Submit survey**.

If you try to submit before finishing required questions, the form will tell you exactly which ones are still needed.

---

### What types of questions will I see?

| Question type | How to answer |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Text** | Type your response in the box |
| **Rating** | Enter a number from 1 to 5 |
| **Multiple choice** | Tick all the options that apply |
| **Single choice** | Pick one option from the dropdown |

---

### I don't see any surveys. Is that normal?

Yes — surveys only appear when your church has published an active one. When there are none, the page will say "No active surveys." Check back later or ask your pastor or admin if you're expecting one.

---

## Common Problems

### The portal says "Loading your portal…" and never finishes.

Try refreshing the page. If it keeps happening, sign out and sign back in. If it still doesn't load, contact your church admin — there may be a connection issue with your account.

---

### I can access the portal but I can't see my usual tabs.

Some sections (like Events or Volunteer) depend on which features your church has switched on. If you expect to see something that isn't there, ask your church admin whether that feature is active for your church.

---

### I signed in but it redirected me to a different page called "Admin console."

This means your account has staff or admin access. The portal is for congregation members — people with admin accounts are taken to the admin workspace automatically. If this is wrong (you're a member, not staff), let your church admin know so they can adjust your access level.

---

### I need to update my name or email but I can't edit those fields.

Names and email addresses are managed by your church admin to keep records accurate. Send your admin a message or speak to them directly, and they can update it for you.

---

### Who do I contact if something isn't working?

First, try your church admin or the staff member who invited you to the portal. They can fix most account issues. For technical problems that your admin can't resolve, they can escalate to FaithFlow AI support through the admin console.
