# ChurchTrack language

ChurchTrack serves single congregations and multi-site church networks globally. These terms separate the customer account, oversight structure, operating congregations, and meeting sites.

## Language

**Workspace**:
A customer's isolated ChurchTrack account, including its people, access, and subscription.
_Avoid_: Church, branch

**Organization**:
A church network or ministry body within a workspace, such as ChurchTrack Organization. It may contain several churches.
_Avoid_: Branch, campus

**Church**:
A congregation with its own members, staff, events, and giving records. Its organization may call it a branch, campus church, assembly, or parish.
_Avoid_: Oversight unit, meeting site

**Oversight unit (planned)**:
A headquarters, region, district, or similar grouping whose leaders supervise churches or other oversight units.
_Avoid_: Church, campus

**Campus**:
A named meeting site within one church, sharing that church's member and financial records.
_Avoid_: Independent campus church, region

**Headquarters office**:
The leadership or administrative center of a network. It is distinct from a headquarters congregation, even when they share a location.
_Avoid_: HQ church

**Church slug**:
A short, readable identifier for a church in public links, such as `churchtrack-organization-hq`.
_Avoid_: Church name
