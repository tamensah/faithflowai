# ChurchTrack language

These terms distinguish a ministry network from the congregations and sites it operates.

## Language

**Workspace**:
A customer's isolated ChurchTrack account, including its people, access, and subscription.
_Avoid_: Church, branch

**Organization**:
A church network or ministry body within a workspace, such as Winners Chapel Ghana. It may contain several churches.
_Avoid_: Branch, campus

**Church**:
A congregation or branch with its own members, staff, events, and giving records. Several churches may belong to one organization.
_Avoid_: Region, administrative headquarters

**Campus**:
A named ministry site within one church. It shares that church's operational boundary.
_Avoid_: Region, independent branch

**Headquarters office**:
The leadership or administrative center of a network. It is distinct from a headquarters congregation, even when they share a location.
_Avoid_: HQ church

**Church slug**:
A short, readable identifier for a church in public links, such as `winners-chapel-ghana-hq`.
_Avoid_: Church name
